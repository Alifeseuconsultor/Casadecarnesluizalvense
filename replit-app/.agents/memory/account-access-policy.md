---
name: Account access policy
description: The owner's chosen first-admin and staff-role onboarding model.
---

The first public registration receives administrator access. Later accounts start as customers, and an administrator may grant administrator or store-operator access. Operators handle orders and preparation; customers use the public storefront.

**Why:** This is the owner's selected onboarding policy. Because registration is public, the first person to register can become an administrator.

**How to apply:** Preserve server-side role checks. In each environment, ensure the intended owner registers first; development and production accounts are separate.
